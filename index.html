        Settings

        Apple Health: a web page can’t read the Health app directly, so enter steps by hand or use the manual import in Settings. Your records stay in this browser on this device. This standalone app does not sync with Claude or Apple Health automatically. Export a backup regularly.

        Install on iPhone & protect your data
        1. Host this folder on HTTPS (for example, GitHub Pages).
        2. Open the address in Safari on iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
        3. Open ReComp once while online to prepare offline use.
        4. Under Backup & restore, tap Download backup regularly. Save the JSON in iCloud Drive or Files.
        5. To move phones, install ReComp at the same web address and restore the JSON backup.

        The app only stores records locally. Clearing website data or deleting the app may erase them. Offline charts and fonts require an initial online load.

        Calibration

        Based on the Mifflin-St Jeor formula, with a 15% deficit for fat loss with muscle gain, 20% for a faster cut and 10% surplus for a lean bulk. Protein is set by bodyweight and goal, and fibre at 14 g per 1,000 kcal (kept between 25 and 40 g). Calories never go below a safe floor. Reassess every 2–3 weeks from your weight trend.

        Support and help

        How is my calorie target calculated?

        The Mifflin-St Jeor formula estimates your resting burn from sex, age, height and weight. That is multiplied by your activity level, then adjusted for your goal: 15% below for fat loss with muscle gain, 20% below for a faster cut, 10% above for a lean bulk. Protein is set per kg of bodyweight. You can override the calories in Settings.

        How does the food lookup work?

        When you tap Calculate, Claude estimates calories, macros and 13 micronutrients for your food and quantity, based on typical Indian home-style recipes. It is an estimate. Weigh food or read the label when precision matters, and use manual entry for packaged food.

        Where is my data stored?

        In your browser on this device and, when you are signed in to Claude, in a private store tied to your account. The line at the bottom of the page says which one is active.

        Why can’t steps sync from my Health app?

        A web page isn’t allowed to read Apple Health, so you type your steps in. The step card on Home has a quick-save field.

        What does Widget view do?

        The VIEW button in the top bar shows only the four stat cards. Open the page with #targets on the end of the link to start in that view. A true home-screen widget isn’t possible for a web page.

        How do streaks, XP and coins work?

        A day counts toward your streak when you complete 3 of your 6 daily quests. Quests and logged food earn XP, every 10 XP gives a coin, and each achievement adds 50 coins to spend in the reward shop.

        This is a personal page, so there is no support desk. Ask the AI coach for nutrition questions.
